Hop Bunny Slide — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hop Bunny Slide (the "Game"), published on Google Play by Tugrion ("we", "us"), handles information. We respect your privacy: the Game does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, coins, costumes, settings and similar) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to our servers. If you uninstall the Game, this data is deleted.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

Data typeExamplesPurpose
Approximate locationEstimated from your IP addressAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Device or other identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
App interactionsApp launches, taps, ad viewsAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
DiagnosticsApp and SDK performance informationAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention

This data is encrypted in transit. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. In-app purchases

If the Game offers optional purchases (for example coin packs or removing ads), payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play's privacy policy applies to those payments.

4. Game engine

The Game is built with the Unity game engine. Unity may process limited technical information (such as device model, operating system version and crash information) to keep the Game running properly. Learn more: Unity Privacy Policy.

Children

The Game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and data deletion

Because we do not run user accounts or store your data on our own servers, there is no personal profile for us to delete. You can delete all game data by uninstalling the Game, and manage advertising data in your device's Google settings as described above. For any request, write to us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date it was last updated.
