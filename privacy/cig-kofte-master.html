Çiğ Köfte Master — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Çiğ Köfte Master (the "Game"), published by Tugrion ("we", "us") on Google Play and on the web, handles information. The Game does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (day, money, upgrades, shop name, settings and similar) is saved only on your device (or, in the web version, in your browser's storage). It is not sent to our servers. If you uninstall the Game or clear your browser data, this data is deleted. The shop name you type in the Game stays on your device.

2. No advertising, no tracking

The Game does not show ads and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not collect advertising IDs and we do not track you across apps or websites.

3. Web portals

If you play the web version on a third-party games website (for example CrazyGames), that website may save your progress to your account on that website and applies its own privacy policy.

4. Game engine

The Game is built with the Unity game engine. Unity may process limited technical information (such as device model, operating system version and crash information) to keep the Game running properly. Learn more: Unity Privacy Policy.

Children

The Game is suitable for all ages. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, please contact us.

Your choices and data deletion

Because we do not run user accounts or store your data on our own servers, there is no personal profile for us to delete. You can delete all game data by uninstalling the Game (or clearing your browser data for the web version). For any request, write to us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date it was last updated.
