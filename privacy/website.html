Website Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you visit tugrion.com (the "Website"), operated by Tugrion ("we", "us"). The privacy policies of our games are separate and are linked at the bottom of every page.

1. No accounts, no forms

The Website does not have user accounts, sign-in, comment forms or newsletters, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details. If you email us at info@tugrion.com, we use your message only to reply to you.

2. Information processed when you visit

ServiceWhat is processedWhy
GitHub Pages (hosting) and Cloudflare (domain name)Technical data such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, in server logs kept by these providersTo deliver the Website securely
Google FontsYour IP address and browser information when the fonts loadTo display the Website's typefaces
Visit counter (Abacus, abacus.jasoncameron.dev)An anonymous request that adds one to a total count. Your IP address is visible to the service when the request is made; no identifier about you is stored by usTo show the total number of site visits
Google AdSense (when ads are enabled)Cookies and similar technologies, device and browser information, approximate location and ad interactionsTo show and measure ads that keep the Website free

3. Storage on your device

We store two small values in your browser's local storage: your chosen language (tg_lang) and the date your visit was last counted (tg_counted), so refreshing a page does not add extra visits. These values never leave your device and contain no personal information. You can delete them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

4. Advertising and cookies

When advertising is enabled, the Website shows ads provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland will be asked for consent through a Google-certified consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from that message, opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

5. Children

The Website is suitable for all ages and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Ads shown to visitors identified as children are limited to non-personalized ads in line with Google's policies.

6. Security

The Website is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections and uses a strict content security policy. It has no database or login system that could expose personal data. If you believe you have found a security issue, please contact us at info@tugrion.com.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not store personal data about visitors, most requests concern the third-party services listed above; we will gladly help you reach them. Contact us at info@tugrion.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page with its date.
