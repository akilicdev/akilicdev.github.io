Çiğ Köfte Master

Play free in your browser · Phone, tablet and computer

Play now in your browser

Çiğ Köfte Master is a cooking and business game about one of Türkiye's best-loved street foods. Knead the köfte, wrap it in lavash with fresh toppings, pack it and hand it to hungry customers — then turn your little street stall into a shop, a restaurant and a brand with branches all over the city.

It runs directly in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. There is nothing to install and no account to create. Your progress is saved automatically in your browser.

The vegetarian çiğ köfte in this game is made without meat — just like the çiğ köfte sold on Turkish streets today.

Artwork

Features

  • From stall to brand — start with a street stall, move into a shop with a marble counter, open a restaurant and then branches across the city.
  • Hands-on cooking — knead the köfte, roll the lavash, add lettuce, parsley, pickles, lemon and pomegranate syrup, then drag the wrap onto the paper to pack it. Later, an automatic packer does it for you.
  • Extra flavours — unlock radish, walnut, crisps and sun-dried tomato as new toppings.
  • Lively customers — students, workers, grandmas, football fans, doctors and more. Some are regulars, some are in a hurry, some change their mind.
  • Run the business — hire staff, set prices, buy from the market, sign wholesale deals, run ads and open a VIP club.
  • Story and goals — ten story chapters, daily tasks, achievements and an endless mode after the finale.
  • 29 languages — including Turkish, Azerbaijani, Kazakh, Uzbek, Kyrgyz, Turkmen, Tatar and most European languages.

How to play

  1. Open Çiğ Köfte Master and start a new game. On a phone, turn it sideways.
  2. Look at the customer's order bubble and tap the toppings in the right order.
  3. Press Roll, then Stop when the marker is in the green zone.
  4. Drag the wrap onto the paper to pack it and tap the customer to serve.
  5. At the end of each day, spend your earnings on upgrades and grow your business.

On a computer you can also use the keyboard: Space rolls, P packs, Esc pauses and F11 switches to full screen.

Frequently asked questions

Is Çiğ Köfte Master free?

Yes. You can play it free in your browser.

Which devices can I play on?

Any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Phones work best in landscape mode.

Do I need an account?

No. Your game is saved automatically in your browser on this device. If you clear your browser data, the save is removed too.

Does the game collect personal data?

No. The game does not ask for any personal information and keeps your progress only on your own device.
