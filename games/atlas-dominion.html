Atlas Dominion

Play free in your browser · Coming soon to Google Play

Play now in your browser

Atlas Dominion is a turn-based grand strategy game played on a real map of the world. Pick a nation, grow its economy, raise armies, forge alliances and try to become the greatest power on Earth — from the year 1200 all the way to a fictional 2027.

It runs directly in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Screenshots

Features

  • A real world map — more than 190 countries and thousands of cities. Every province has its own population, economy and resources.
  • Ten eras — 1200, 1300, 1350, 1400, 1650, 1800, 1914, 1919, 1939 and 2027. Read about them in Ten Eras, One Map.
  • War and peace — move armies across borders, take cities, then sit at the peace table and set your terms.
  • Diplomacy — alliances, non-aggression pacts, envoys, military access, trade in cities, and even your own union with its own name and flag. Be too aggressive and the world may unite against you.
  • Run your country — set taxes, keep provinces stable, fight epidemics and invest in mines, farms and industry.
  • Technology and space — research land, air and naval units, mining and industry; launch satellites and send a mission to the Moon.
  • Play with friends — host a room, share the code and play together in the same world.
  • Saves and rankings — automatic saving, cloud backups you can continue on another device, and rankings for economy, technology, population and prestige.
  • 9 languages — English, Turkish, Arabic, Chinese, Italian, Spanish, German, French and Russian.

How to start

  1. Open Atlas Dominion and start a new game.
  2. Choose an era, then tap the country you want to lead.
  3. Use the panels at the top — Wars, Technology, Maps, Ranking, Investment and News — to manage your nation.
  4. Tap another country to open diplomacy: send an envoy, request an alliance or, if you must, declare war.
  5. When you are ready, press End Turn. Each turn the world moves forward and every country acts.

New to strategy games? Our beginner's guide to your first campaign walks you through the first twenty turns.

All scenarios and events in Atlas Dominion are fictional. The game is a strategy simulation and does not represent the views of Tugrion on any real country, people or conflict.

Frequently asked questions

Is Atlas Dominion free?

Yes. You can play it free in your browser. The upcoming Android version will be free too, with optional rewarded ads and a one-time Supporter Pack.

Which devices can I play on?

Any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. On phones we recommend landscape mode for the best view of the map.

Do I need an account?

No. Your game is saved automatically in your browser. Cloud saves give you a code that lets you continue on another device.

How do I play with friends?

One player hosts a room and shares the room code. Friends enter the code to join the same world. In multiplayer, time flows continuously for everyone instead of in turns.

Is there an Android app?

The Android version is currently in testing on Google Play. Follow us to hear when it launches.

How is my data handled?

Please see the Atlas Dominion privacy policy. You can request deletion of cloud data on the data deletion page.
