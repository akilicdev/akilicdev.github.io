Hop Bunny: Jump, Run, Collect

Available on Android · Free

Get it on Google Play

Hop Bunny is a colorful, one-finger platform runner starring Hopi, a brave little bunny with a red scarf. It is easy enough for young players to pick up in seconds, yet the later worlds ask for real timing and skill.

Screenshots

Features

  • 40 levels in 5 worlds — Carrot Meadow, Mushroom Forest, Sand Dunes, Ice Mountains and Fire Valley, each with its own obstacles and creatures.
  • Three stars per level — reach the flag, collect 60% of the carrots and lose at most one heart.
  • Power-ups — Magnet, Shield and 2x Score, plus shop upgrades that make them last longer.
  • Endless Run — a never-ending track that slowly gets harder. How far can you go?
  • Daily rewards and Star Chests — come back each day and spend your stars on chests.
  • Colors for Hopi — unlock new looks, from classic white to rainbow and golden.
  • English and Turkish — the game follows your phone's language.

How to play

  1. Turn your phone sideways — Hop Bunny is played in landscape mode.
  2. Tap to jump, tap again in the air to double jump, and hold to jump higher.
  3. Land on top of most creatures to stomp them. Hedgehogs, scorpions and snowmen cannot be stomped — jump over them.
  4. Follow the carrots: they usually show the safest path through a level.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hop Bunny free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play. It shows ads between levels, and you can choose to watch a rewarded ad for bonuses such as double rewards.

Are there in-app purchases?

Optional coin packs and a one-time purchase that removes ads between levels are available. Every level can be completed without paying. Purchases are processed by Google Play.

Is it suitable for children?

Hop Bunny is designed to be friendly for all ages: no violence beyond cartoon stomps, no chat and no account. We recommend parents turn on purchase authentication in the Play Store.

Does it work offline?

The levels can be played without an internet connection. Ads and purchases need a connection.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones and tablets. An iOS version is not available yet.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Uninstalling the game deletes your progress.

How is my data handled?

Please see the Hop Bunny privacy policy.
