Word Search XXL

Coming soon to Google Play

Follow for launch news

Word Search XXL is a calm, friendly word search game designed to be easy on the eyes. The letters are big, the colors are clear and there is no rush — perfect for a relaxing break, for training your brain every day, and for playing together with the whole family. It is coming soon to Google Play.

Eight beautiful themes

Features

  • Extra-large, easy-to-read letters — choose Normal, Large or Extra large text, with a high-contrast option and a clear, highly legible font.
  • A journey across 24 cities — travel from Istanbul to Paris, Rome and beyond, and collect a postcard for your Travel Album at every stop.
  • Eight themes and 150+ words each — Animals, Food, Nature, Beach & Sea, Travel, Home, Garden and Winter.
  • Seven languages — English, German, French, Dutch, Spanish, Italian and Turkish, with the right letters for each language.
  • Three puzzle styles — classic grids, shaped puzzles (heart, diamond, circle, plus and triangle) and mystery puzzles.
  • Daily Puzzle and Proverb of the Day — a fresh challenge every day, plus a famous saying with missing words to complete.
  • Hidden Treasure — find real words that are not on the list to fill a treasure chest.
  • Relax mode — no timer, and every puzzle earns three stars.
  • Read aloud — found words can be spoken out loud.

Family & Friends

Create your own puzzle with up to ten words — the names of your grandchildren, your hometown, your favorite dishes — and share it with a five-digit code. Your family enters the code and tries to beat your time. You can also send a friend the code of any puzzle you solved and compare results.

Train your brain

After some puzzles a short Memory Check asks which three words were in the grid. Every week you get a Weekly Brain Report with the words you found, puzzles solved, days played and your average time — and you can send it to your family.

How to play

  1. Look at the word list under the grid.
  2. Drag your finger across the letters to mark a word. Words can run across, down or diagonally.
  3. Stuck? Use a bulb hint to reveal the first letter of a word.
  4. Find every word to finish the level, earn stars and move on along your journey.

Frequently asked questions

When will Word Search XXL be released?

Soon! The game is in its final stage of testing and will arrive on Google Play shortly. Follow us on social media or check our blog for the launch date.

Is it free?

Yes. Word Search XXL will be free to play, supported by ads. An optional purchase removes banners and ads between levels; reward videos always stay optional.

Who is it for?

Everyone who enjoys words — and especially players who prefer large, clear letters and a calm pace without pressure.

Which languages are supported?

English, German, French, Dutch, Spanish, Italian and Turkish. The game starts in your phone's language, and you can change it in Settings.
