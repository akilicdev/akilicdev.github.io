Hop Bunny Slide

Coming soon to Google Play

Follow for launch news

Hopi is back — this time on a sled! Hop Bunny Slide is a fast 3D downhill racer with curves, ramps and giant slingshots. It is currently in closed testing and will be available on Google Play soon.

The six worlds

What to expect

  • Six worlds of downhill tracks with hills, curves and ramps.
  • Five fruit and vegetable sleds — cabbage, carrot, pumpkin, watermelon and a royal pineapple.
  • A garage to grow — upgrade each sled from level 1 to 10 and watch it change as it grows.
  • Durability that matters — crashes damage your sled, but you can always finish the run and repair it later.
  • Plant slingshots that launch Hopi into the air, with upgrades of their own.
  • Hopi's Farm — a small income that grows while you are away.
  • Endless road through regions like Spring Valley, Village Road, Coast, Swamp, City and Highway — with day and night, rain, fog, snow, sandstorms and avalanches.
  • Rides and planes — swap your sled for an Eggplant Snowmobile, Strawberry Kart or Banana Hoverboard, and take to the sky with the Pear Plane while dodging meteors, birds and lightning.
  • A wardrobe for Hopi — costumes such as Pirate, Ninja, Knight, Wizard, Astronaut and Superhero.

How to play

  1. Pull back and release the slingshot. Let go when the power meter is above 90% for a perfect launch.
  2. Steer left and right with your finger while sliding to collect coins and avoid obstacles.
  3. Jump and use the rocket at the right moment to clear gaps and fly further.
  4. Spend your coins in the garage on stronger sleds, slingshots and repairs, then go again.
