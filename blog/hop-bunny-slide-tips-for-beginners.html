Hop Bunny Slide: 7 Tips for Your First Day on the Slopes

New to Hop Bunny Slide? These seven simple tips will help Hopi launch further, crash less and earn more coins from the very first run.

Hop Bunny Slide is now out on Google Play, and lots of new players are taking Hopi down the hill for the first time. The first levels are easy, but a few small habits make a big difference later on. Here are seven tips we wish every new player knew.

1. Master the perfect launch

Every run starts at the giant slingshot. Pull back, watch the power meter and let go when it is above 90%. A perfect launch gives Hopi a strong start and saves you precious seconds on longer tracks. If you miss it, don't worry — just aim for it again next run.

2. Steer early, not late

Hills and sharp curves hide what is coming next. Move your finger a little before you reach an obstacle instead of at the last moment. Small, smooth movements keep Hopi on the slide lane and away from rocks and fences.

3. Save the jump and the rocket for the right moment

Jumping clears gaps and low obstacles, and the rocket helps you fly over the hardest parts. Using them too early often means landing right in front of the next problem. Look ahead, then jump or boost when the track really needs it.

4. Collect coins, but not at any price

Carrot coins pay for everything in the garage, so grab them when it is safe. But a crash costs more than a few coins: it damages your sled. When a row of coins sits next to an obstacle, it is usually smarter to let a few go.

5. Take care of your sled

Each sled has its own level and durability. Crashes wear it down, and a damaged sled slows down and loses power. Visit the garage regularly to repair it — the first repair is free — and level up the sled you use most. A well-kept sled is the easiest way to beat a level you are stuck on.

6. Upgrade your slingshot too

Many players only upgrade sleds and forget the slingshot. A stronger slingshot means a faster start on every single run, which adds up quickly on the long tracks of the later worlds.

7. Let Hopi's Farm work for you

Hopi's Farm earns coins even while you are not playing. Collect your harvest when you open the game, finish the daily tasks, and you will always have something to spend in the garage. When you have finished a few levels, try the Endless mode to chase your best distance.

Six worlds are waiting

From Carrot Valley and Candy Land to the Snowy Mountains and the Riverside finale, Hop Bunny Slide has 60 levels to explore. Take your time, enjoy the ride — and let us know how far you got!
