Contact us

We read every message.

Tugrion is a small, independent studio. The fastest way to reach us is by email: info@tugrion.com. We usually reply within a few working days.

What you can write to us about

  • Game support — a bug, a lost purchase or a question about one of our games. Please include the game name, your device model and Android version.
  • Feedback and ideas — tell us what you love and what we should improve.
  • Privacy requests — questions about data or deletion requests. See our website privacy policy and the privacy policy of each game.
  • Business and press — partnerships, publishing, licensing or media enquiries.
  • Security — if you believe you have found a security issue, please email us; see also our security.txt.

Purchases and refunds

All in-app purchases in our games are processed by Google Play. For refunds, please use Google Play's refund request process; we are also happy to help if you email us.
